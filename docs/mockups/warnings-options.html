Warning display options

Corner cuts (PENA 7), off-track passes (8, 9) and track limits (27 to 29) add to one count. Every third warning (3, 6, 9) gives a 3 s penalty. Collisions (4) do not count. Payload today: race.infringements = {corner_cuts, warnings, penalty_s, warn_at} and track_warning_kind for the latest warning only. Each preview shows the full dashboard at count 2, crops at counts 1, 2 and 3, and the phone at count 3.
